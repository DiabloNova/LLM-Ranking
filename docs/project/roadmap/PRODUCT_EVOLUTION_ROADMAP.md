Document: Product Evolution & Revenue Roadmap
Version: 1.0
Status: Planned

Repository: "seorchable"
Primary Goal: Transform Seorchable into a unified, revenue-generating AI Search Visibility, SEO, GEO/AEO, Content Intelligence, and Competitive Intelligence platform.

---

1. Executive Vision

Seorchable will evolve from a collection of individual SEO/AEO tools into a unified SaaS intelligence platform.

The product will provide one authenticated workspace where users can discover, evaluate, monitor, optimize, and improve their visibility across:

- Traditional Search Engines
- AI Search Engines
- LLM-powered answer systems
- Brand and citation ecosystems
- Competitor search visibility
- Technical SEO
- Content quality and optimization
- Structured data and entities
- Knowledge graphs
- AI-generated answers
- Search and AI performance analytics

The core product principle is:

«One account → One workspace → One unified dashboard → Multiple intelligence tools → Free entry → Paid expansion.»

Seorchable should not be positioned as "another SEO tool."

The primary positioning should be:

«An AI Search Visibility & SEO Intelligence Platform that helps businesses understand, measure, and improve how they appear in search engines and AI-powered answer systems.»

7. Dashboard Philosophy

The dashboard should not simply be a collection of links to tools.

It should become the user's operational command center.

The dashboard should answer:

1. How visible is my website?
2. How healthy is my SEO?
3. How visible is my brand to AI systems?
4. Where am I losing visibility?
5. Which competitors are outperforming me?
6. Which pages need improvement?
7. Which content should I create?
8. Which technical problems need fixing?
9. Which AI systems mention my brand?
10. What should I do next?
